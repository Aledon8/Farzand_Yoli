# Farzand Yoli

**Slogan**: Parents are not born, they are made.

## 🚀 Description

Farzand Yoli is a comprehensive platform designed to support parents in providing the best conditions for their children's education, development, and career. We address key problems in modern education by offering simple and accessible tools for both parents and children.

## 💡 Problems We Solve

Modern parents face many challenges related to their children's education and development:

- **Fragmented Information**: Finding reliable kindergartens, schools, lyceums, and universities is increasingly difficult due to the lack of a centralized, trusted information source.
- **Budget Constraints**: Parents struggle to track and optimize expenses related to education, transportation, meals, and extracurricular activities.
- **Uncertainty in Decision-Making**: Without personalized guidance, it’s hard to determine the best educational path for a child’s unique abilities and needs.
- **Limited Family Time**: Many parents find it challenging to balance work, education planning, and nurturing family bonds.
- **Lack of Career Guidance**: Children often miss out on early career planning tools, leading to uncertainty about future goals and responsibilities.

## 🛠️ Our Solution

Farzand Yoli provides a range of tools and solutions for parents and children to help address these challenges:

1. **Educational Institution Finder**: A centralized database built on open data that allows parents to search and compare kindergartens, schools, lyceums, and universities with filters for type, location, rating, and reviews.
   
2. **Family Budget Management**: A built-in calculator that helps parents manage and optimize education-related expenses, including transportation and extracurricular activities.

3. **Personalized Recommendations**: A parent-child test that provides tailored advice on the best educational paths and activities based on the child's needs and interests.

4. **Strengthening Family Values**: Tools like family games, advice columns, and event planners that help parents build strong family connections.

5. **Career Navigator**: Guides children through career exploration, helping them discover their talents, develop skills, and plan for the future.

6. **Responsibility Training for Children**: Interactive tasks and programs that teach children responsibility and self-discipline.

## ⚙️ Implementation

1. **Data Aggregation**: Collaborate with educational institutions and leverage open data sources to create an accurate and comprehensive database.

2. **Platform Development**: Build a user-friendly web and mobile platform with advanced filters, a budget calculator, and interactive features.

3. **Personalization Algorithms**: Integrate AI-powered recommendation systems to provide tailored advice on educational paths and career guidance.

4. **Marketing and Outreach**: Launch targeted campaigns in schools, parenting communities, and on social media to introduce the platform to families.
