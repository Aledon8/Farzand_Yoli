# Farzand Yoli

**Slogan**: Parents are not born, they are made.

## 🚀 Description

Farzand Yoli is a comprehensive platform designed to support parents in providing the best conditions for their children's education, development, and career. We address key problems in modern education by offering simple and accessible tools for both parents and children.

---

## 💡 Problems We Solve

Modern parents face many challenges related to their children's education and development:

- **Fragmented Information**: Difficulty finding reliable kindergartens, schools, lyceums, and universities due to a lack of centralized, trusted sources.
- **Budget Constraints**: Struggles in tracking and optimizing expenses for education, transportation, meals, and extracurricular activities.
- **Uncertainty in Decision-Making**: Lack of personalized guidance for determining the best educational path for a child's unique needs.
- **Limited Family Time**: Balancing work, education planning, and family bonding can be challenging.
- **Lack of Career Guidance**: Children often miss early career planning tools, leading to uncertainty about their future.

---

## 🛠️ Our Solution

Farzand Yoli provides tools and solutions for parents and children:

1. **Educational Institution Finder**: A centralized database for searching and comparing kindergartens, schools, lyceums, and universities.
2. **Family Budget Management**: A calculator to manage and optimize education-related expenses.
3. **Personalized Recommendations**: Tailored advice on educational paths based on a parent-child test.
4. **Strengthening Family Values**: Tools for family games, advice columns, and event planning.
5. **Career Navigator**: Helps children explore careers and plan for their future.
6. **Responsibility Training for Children**: Interactive tasks and programs to teach responsibility.

---

## ⚙️ Implementation

### Backend
- **Python (Django)**: Core backend framework for API services, database management, and admin panel.
- **Django REST Framework (DRF)**: For building RESTful APIs.

### Frontend
- **JavaScript (React)**: To build a dynamic, interactive interface.
- **Bootstrap**: For responsive and consistent UI design.

### Database
- **PostgreSQL**: To store and manage structured data like user profiles, educational institutions, and expenses.
